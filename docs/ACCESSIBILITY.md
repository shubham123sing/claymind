# Accessibility (V6 4.6): what is supported, and what is not

For the submission text. Checked in the emulator with scripted hands; **none of it is verified on a
Quest** (no device).

## Supported
- **Seated, hands only.** Everything is within reach of a seated player at a 0.72 m virtual table;
  no walking, no controllers, no microphone, no voice.
- **One-handed menu** (Settings): the menu floats beside the clay, so no palm-up gesture is needed;
  every panel can be poked with either hand.
- **Left-handed** (Settings): the left hand sculpts and catches, the right palm opens the menu.
- **Large text** (Settings, new in Stage D): every headset panel 20% bigger again (text scale
  1.25 -> 1.5); checked with no overlapping panels.
- **High contrast** (Settings): opaque dark panels, white text, thicker outlines.
- **Reduced motion** (Settings): no bobbing, no clay breathing, menus appear without easing.
- **Colour is never the only signal.** Hover and grab highlights are a white brightening of the
  object itself (works for every colour vision); settings switches also move their knob; the score
  is a number, a sound and an edge light.
- **No flashing.** Every highlight is one fade of under a second; nothing blinks or strobes.
- **Sound is optional** (Settings: Sound off); every sound has a visual partner (glow, toast, number).
- **Captions:** the app does not speak; all messages are on-screen text.
- **Comfort:** no artificial locomotion, no camera motion, an opaque VR room by default (mixed reality
  optional); Recenter (Bring to life -> Recenter) puts the clay back in front of you.
- **Movable panels** (gap 1): pinch the small bar above any panel and drag it where it is easier to
  reach (up to 60 cm from its place); Recenter puts every panel back.
- **Performance mode** (Settings): simpler effects for a steadier frame rate.
- **Autosave:** a crash or a closed page loses nothing.

## Not supported (honest)
- No seated-height calibration: the table height is fixed.
- No controller fallback, no voice, no switch access, no screen reader.
- Colour-blind palettes for the clay colours themselves are not offered (the player chooses colours).
- Text size, reach and target size are not checked on a real headset.
